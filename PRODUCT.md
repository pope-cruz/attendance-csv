# Product

## Register

product

## Users

tech@nyu operators and community organizers who need to review event attendance exports quickly and accurately during routine internal operations.

## Product Purpose

Import Luma and NYU Engage CSV exports, preserve their source data, surface validation issues, and add enough event context to support reliable attendance records and future member history.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, and pragmatic. The interface should feel calm and operational, with confidence coming from legibility and predictable behavior rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

Not a SaaS marketing surface, lifestyle dashboard, or pastel card showcase. Avoid oversized hero copy, decorative status colors, floating glassy panels, excessive rounding, and visual hierarchy that delays the primary task.

## Design Principles

- Put the attendance workflow before explanation.
- Make correctness and reviewability visible.
- Keep secondary context available without letting it dominate.
- Prefer familiar controls and concise language.
- Preserve source truth and never hide ambiguity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline. Maintain visible keyboard focus, semantic labels, sufficient text and control contrast, non-color status cues, comfortable touch targets, and reduced-motion behavior.
